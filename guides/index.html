1Today's tip

If you want to perform at a local venue, start by hanging out there regularly. Get to know the folks, show some support, and they'll be more likely to support you.

To do today Look at the calendar of your favorite local venue, pick a show to attend, and get to know some of the staff while you're there.

The Independent Artist Release Checklist

135 steps, in order, from 12 weeks before release to the weeks after it comes out. Print it, check things off, and stop worrying about what you forgot.

  1. Phase 1 · 10 to 12 weeks out

    Strategy

    Release date, single vs. EP vs. album, audience, marketing plan and ad budget.

  2. Phase 2

    Business and legal

    PRO, The MLC and SoundExchange registrations, copyright, split sheets, sample clearance, ISRC codes.

  3. Phase 3

    Create all assets

    Masters, cover art, photos, videos, Spotify Canvas, bios, press release and EPK.

  4. Phase 4 · 8 weeks out

    Digital setup

    Distributor upload, metadata, smart link, pre-save, and every artist profile updated.

  5. Phase 5 · 6 to 8 weeks out

    Marketing

    Spotify editorial pitch, press and playlist lists, email campaign, ads and trackable links.

  6. Phase 6

    Social content

    20 to 30 posts, Reels, TikToks, Shorts, teasers, countdown and release-day graphics.

  7. Phase 7 · 2 weeks out

    Final checks

    Email, captions and hashtags ready, smart link tested, ads approved, team notified.

  8. Release day and after

    Launch and follow through

    Every platform to post on, what to verify in streaming, and how to keep the song moving after week one.
